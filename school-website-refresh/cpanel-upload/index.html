About Mary Royal School

A Center of Academic Rigor & Ethical Foundation

We are dedicated to equipping every learner with critical thinking, moral integrity, digital literacy, and practical skills required to excel in modern society.

Our Vision

To be a premier institution recognized for nurturing well-rounded, innovative, and ethically grounded global citizens.

Our Mission

Providing a dynamic, child-centered education that fosters academic excellence, talent development, and strong moral principles.

Core Values

Integrity, Respect, Continuous Improvement, Innovation, Compassion, and Teamwork guide every action in our school community.

Personalized Mentorship

Dedicated Educators Who Care For Every Child

Our qualified teaching staff ensures that no learner is left behind. Through individual attention, practical exercises, and psychological support, we create a thriving environment for academic and emotional resilience.

Educational Pathways

Structured Learning at Every Stage

From foundation years to junior secondary, our curriculum builds confidence and competence.

Playgroup - PP2

Early Years Education (EYE)

Play-based and activity-driven learning designed to spark curiosity, social skills, language acquisition, and motor coordination.

  • Phonics & Early Literacy
  • Creative Arts & Music Play
  • Safe Playground Facilities
Grade 1 - Grade 6

Primary School (CBC)

Comprehensive CBC framework laying firm foundations in Mathematics, Science, Social Studies, ICT, and Languages.

  • Interactive Computer Labs
  • STEM Practical Projects
  • Languages (English, Kiswahili, French)
Grade 7 - Grade 9

Junior Secondary School

Advanced learning pathways featuring integrated science laboratories, technical skills, leadership, and career guidance.

  • Fully Equipped Science Labs
  • Career Mentorship & Counseling
  • Leadership & Public Speaking
