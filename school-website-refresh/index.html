About Our School

Where Every Child Discovers Their Royal Potential

Mary Royal Primary & Junior School is a leading CBC institution in Obunga, Kisumu, committed to raising confident, competent, and values-driven learners from Playgroup through Junior Secondary Grade 9.

  • Competency-Based Curriculum

    Practical, learner-centred education that builds real-world skills.

  • Small, Caring Class Sizes

    Individual attention so no child is ever left behind.

  • Modern Facilities

    Computer labs, library, and safe, stimulating play environments.

  • Experienced, Passionate Teachers

    CBC-trained educators who mentor with heart.
