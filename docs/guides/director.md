# Director guide

## Sign in
Use `director@maryroyal.ac.ke` with the temporary local password in README. On first sign-in, choose a new password of at least 12 characters.

## Create an account
Open **Accounts** in the desktop sidebar. Enter the person's name, email and role, then select **Create account**. Copy the temporary password shown once and share it privately. The new account must change it at first sign-in. Account list shows whether each account is active and whether it still needs a password change.

Use **Suspend** to block sign-in, **Reactivate** to restore access, **Reset password** to issue a new one-time credential, or **Unlock** after repeated failed logins. Each action is audit recorded. Account profile edits, deletion/archive and bulk imports are not implemented.

## Add a learner
Open **Learners**, choose **Add learner**, complete the school admission number, name, grade and gender, and save. Search by learner name, admission number or grade. Admission numbers must be unique. Learners are archived rather than deleted once records are attached.

This prototype does not yet capture guardians, UPI/NEMIS, DOB, prior school, medical data or full admission details. Use demo records only.
