# Accountant guide

Use `accountant@maryroyal.ac.ke` and the local temporary password in README. Change it at first sign-in. The learner picker exposes only name, admission number and class. In **Finance**, create a term invoice, then record M-Pesa (unique transaction code), cash, bank or cheque payments. Overpayments become learner credit for future invoices. Use the receipt link to print/save a branded receipt. Voids require a reason and are recorded; a payment whose credit was already spent is blocked until reconciliation. Fee templates, waivers, arrears reports, expenses, payroll and statements are not implemented.
