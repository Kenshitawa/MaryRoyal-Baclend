# Secretary guide

Sign in with `secretary@maryroyal.ac.ke` and the fresh-database password in the README, then set a private password.

## Admissions

Open **Learners → Admit learner**, enter the learner’s name, class and gender, then save. The system assigns the next `MR-##` admission number and creates a learner sign-in account with a unique email in the format `mr-##@learners.maryroyal.ac.ke`. The printed credential slip contains a temporary password shown only on creation. Give it privately to the learner’s guardian. For a pending admission, the account stays disabled until the Head Teacher approves it; the learner must change the password at first sign-in. The Director can see the admission and approval audit event.

## Fee payments

Open **Finance**, choose the learner’s invoice and enter the amount and method. M-Pesa payments require a unique transaction code. Saved payments are pending; a Head Teacher or Director confirms them. Only confirmed payments affect the learner balance and make an official receipt available to the family.

## Complaints

Open **Complaints** to record a concern raised by a parent, learner or Head Teacher. Enter the reporter’s name and role, choose a learner when the concern is about a child, and select the receiving teacher or school leader. The receiving staff member and authorized school leaders can reply in the thread. The Director has schoolwide oversight.
